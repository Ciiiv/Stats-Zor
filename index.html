        Team swap

        How this works

        Move the player to another team in place of one of its players. Each player's 5v5 impact (his on-ice rates minus his team's rates with him on the bench) is added to the other team's baseline without the replaced player.

        Part two · deployment · MoneyPuck game-by-game data

        Role and fit

        How the coach used him each night, how he did in each role compared with what that role normally produces, and where his results say he fits best. Each game, forwards are slotted 1st–4th line and defensemen 1st–3rd pair by 5v5 ice time on their team that night; power-play and penalty-kill units come from special-teams ice time.

        Team deployment card

        How this works

        Every regular on a team: where the coach used him at even strength and on special teams, against where his results say he fits. "5v5 fit" uses even strength only; "Overall fit" adds special teams and finishing; "Vs. role" is his 5v5 xGF% minus the average for the slots he played. The even-strength flag uses overall fit.

        Part three · value · Stats-Zor model

        What he's worth

        Stats-Zor's own value model turns 5v5 impact, finishing, power play, penalty kill and penalties into goals and wins above a replacement-level player, then prices those wins with the salary cap. It estimates fair value; it doesn't know actual contracts.

        Part four · careers across eras · NHL data

        Against history

        Raw points from different eras aren't comparable. Era-adjusted figures rescale every season to a league scoring 6.0 goals per game. Advanced stats (expected goals) exist only from 2008-09 on.
