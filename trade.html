Pick two teams and add what each side sends: NHL skaters and goalies, prospects (recent draft picks not yet NHL regulars) and draft picks. Value is the Stats-Zor model's projected wins above replacement over the years each asset is controlled. Add each player's years left on his contract after this season (0 for a rental) to count the rest of his deal, and his cap hit to bring salary into it. Young players' restricted-free-agent years are counted automatically (you can switch that off); the verdict then uses surplus value (what he's worth minus what he's paid).
